This paper studies geometric properties in epipolar plane images (EPIs) for light-field referring image segmentation. We design EPI depth and edge feature extraction modules and use epipolar cues to address limited segmentation accuracy in complex occluded scenes. The paper was accepted as an Oral presentation at ICIG 2026 (CCF-C).
